BiteSprint Privacy Policy

Package ID: com.aistudio.fooddelivery.bspxrq • Effective Date: November 24, 2026 • Developer: Shakti Swarup Mohanty

This Privacy Policy explains how BiteSprint ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Shakti Swarup Mohanty, collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the BiteSprint Android application across Customer, Vendor, and Delivery Driver roles.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Information: Full name, email address, selected role (Customer, Vendor, or Driver), and optional restaurant/hub name provided during registration via Firebase Authentication.
  • Location Information: Approximate and precise GPS location (when you grant ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION or ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) to identify nearby restaurants, set delivery addresses, and display delivery routing.
  • Order & Address Data: Saved delivery addresses, delivery instructions, cart items, payment method preference, and order history stored locally on your device using Android Room database.
  • Photos & Camera (Drivers Only): Delivery drivers may capture or select a delivery confirmation photo as Proof of Delivery when completing an order.
  • Notifications: Permission to post order status notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) on Android 13+ devices.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate users securely using Firebase Authentication and manage role-specific dashboards (Customer, Vendor, Driver).
  • To auto-fill your delivery location and calculate estimated delivery routes and ETAs.
  • To process orders, display live order tracking updates, and send timely order status alerts.
  • To allow delivery drivers to confirm order handoff with visual proof of delivery.

3. Third-Party Services

BiteSprint uses trusted Google services that may process technical or authentication data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase Authentication & App Check (reCAPTCHA): Used for secure account sign-up, sign-in, password reset, and abuse prevention.
  • Google Play Services Location: Used to resolve device coordinates and nearby delivery addresses when permission is granted.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties or use your data for third-party advertising.

4. Data Security & Retention

All authentication traffic is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. Order history, saved addresses, and preferences are stored locally on your device. We retain your authentication profile only for as long as your account remains active or until you request deletion.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your BiteSprint account and all associated local app data at any time:

  • In-App: Open your Profile Menu (top-right avatar) → tap Delete Account → confirm Delete Forever.
  • On the Web: Follow our Account & Data Deletion Page or email shaktiswarup500123@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

BiteSprint is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
