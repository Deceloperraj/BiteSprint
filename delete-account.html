ACCOUNT & DATA DELETION

Delete Your BiteSprint Account

App: BiteSprint (com.aistudio.fooddelivery.bspxrq) • Developer: Shakti Swarup Mohanty

You can permanently delete your BiteSprint account and all associated personal data using either of the two options below.

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the BiteSprint app on your Android device and sign in.
  2. Tap your Profile Avatar in the top-right corner of your dashboard (Customer, Vendor, or Driver).
  3. Select Delete Account from the menu.
  4. Tap Delete Forever in the confirmation dialog.

Option 2: Request Deletion Online (If App Is Uninstalled)

If you no longer have the app installed, submit a deletion request using the form below or email shaktiswarup500123@gmail.com with the subject "BiteSprint Account Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 7 business days.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Deleted Immediately / Upon Request: Your Firebase Authentication user record (email, display name, UID), saved profile preferences, saved delivery addresses, active cart items, local order history, and notifications.
  • Retention: No personal data is retained after account deletion is completed, except where required by applicable law.
